# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-directed traders aged 20-40 who already invest in stocks, ETFs, or crypto. They range from beginner-intermediate to advanced and currently stitch together workflows across TradingView, Yahoo Finance, spreadsheets, broker apps, and Discord or Telegram groups. They want an AI-powered workspace that consolidates research, backtesting, alerts, risk management, portfolio review, and trade journaling.

## Product Purpose

Financial Advisor Agent helps traders turn scattered market inputs into a disciplined investing workflow. It combines AI research, market data, sentiment analysis, portfolio optimization, and future strategy tooling so users can save time, reduce context switching, and make better-documented decisions. Success means the product feels faster and more coherent than the user's current collection of tools while encouraging risk awareness rather than impulsive trading.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, sharp, trustworthy, trader-native. The product should feel like an advanced workspace for people who actively manage capital, not a generic chatbot or entertainment-style trading app. The tone should be concise, clear, and credible, with enough technical confidence for experienced users and enough guidance for newer traders to avoid feeling lost.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark AI dashboard styling, crypto casino energy, navy-and-gold finance cliches, toy-like chat app patterns, vague "AI magic" claims, and interfaces that over-index on glowing cards, gradient text, or decorative glass effects. The product should not feel like a Discord signal group, meme-stock terminal, or thin wrapper around a chat input.

## Design Principles

1. Make discipline the default: risk, assumptions, and next steps should be visible before action.
2. Consolidate without flattening: unify research, alerts, backtests, portfolios, and journals while preserving each workflow's depth.
3. Show evidence, not vibes: AI answers and insights should expose data sources, confidence, caveats, and tradeoffs.
4. Serve both scan and depth: the UI should support fast market checks and focused analysis sessions.
5. Keep the trader in control: automation should assist decisions, not obscure them or create false certainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support reduced motion, keyboard navigation, readable dense layouts, and colorblind-safe financial signals so positive, negative, bullish, bearish, risk, and warning states are not communicated by color alone.
